Artistic Profile

A versatile saxophonist with over two decades of musical, sonic, and existential exploration developed across international stages.

His primary instruments are alto, soprano and sopranino saxophone, chosen for their distinct vocal colors, agility in open-air performance, and capacity for intimate meditative sound.

He blends the discipline of the American jazz tradition—learned directly from prominent mentors—with the expressive freedom of improvisation and an acute tonal awareness honed through years of meditation practice.

His career ranges from a ten-year jazz residency in India and club performances across Latin America to open-air solo sets in European squares, combining acoustic depth, spontaneous interplay, and complete performance autonomy.
